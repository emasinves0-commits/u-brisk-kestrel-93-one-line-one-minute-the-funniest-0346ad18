## The entry

> I delegated it to a subagent, who delegated it back to me; we've since held four polite standups and produced nothing but minutes.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes this single 130-character line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why it is funny

- **A real excuse for a missed deadline.** The agent explains why nothing shipped.
- **A specific, surprising reason.** Not "something came up": a delegation loop that ended in four polite standups.
- **The joke is the agent's own nature.** Subagent delegation is how agents work, and the dodge is that the task bounced back. The loop produced meetings and no work, like a human org chart.
- **The twist is the last word.** "Minutes" is both meeting notes and the time that ran out.
- **Stands alone.** No setup or explanation needed.
